Case studies

What changed after we took over

Three real problems clients brought us, and what the numbers looked like afterwards.

Manufacturing · Malanpur

Night-shift pilferage at a components plant

Challenge

Repeated stock shortfalls on night shift, no reliable gate record, and a previous agency rotating unfamiliar guards every week.

What we did

Fixed 9-guard roster with named night supervisor, gate-pass register tied to dispatch, two surprise night rounds a week, and CCTV at the loading bay.

Result

Pilferage incidents down to zero over 11 months; gate records now accepted in monthly stock audit.

Residential · Gwalior

240-flat society with visitor chaos

Challenge

Unlogged visitors, delivery crowding at the gate, and residents complaining that guards were untrained in dealing with the public.

What we did

Deployed 6 guards plus one supervisor, introduced a visitor log with resident call-confirmation, and ran a soft-skills and emergency drill every quarter.

Result

Gate complaints in the RWA register dropped from 14 a month to under 2; contract renewed twice.

Healthcare · Bhopal

Hospital needing 24×7 cover at short notice

Challenge

Outgoing vendor exited with two weeks' notice, leaving 18 posts across OPD, ICU corridor, parking and mortuary to be filled without a gap.

What we did

Mobilised 18 verified staff in 4 days, briefed each post separately, and assigned a dedicated account manager on-site for the first fortnight.

Result

Zero uncovered shifts during transition; scope later extended to housekeeping.

Questions

The things clients actually ask first

Absence is escalated the moment the shift-start call goes unanswered. Our supervisor deploys a reliever from the local standby pool — the commitment is a replacement on post within 12 hours, and for critical single-guard posts we aim for the same shift.

You are never billed for an uncovered shift. Absent days are deducted from the monthly invoice and shown as a separate line, not silently absorbed.

Standard contracts are billed monthly in arrears, against the attendance register signed by your site in-charge. The invoice shows per-post rate, actual days worked, overtime if any, statutory contributions (PF/ESIC) and GST as separate lines — so nothing is bundled into one opaque figure.

Short-term and event deployments are billed per shift, usually with part-payment in advance. Payment terms are typically 15–30 days from invoice; we can align to your procurement cycle.

For standard requirements in our covered cities, verified staff are on post within 48 hours of a confirmed order. Large mobilisations (20+ personnel) or specialised roles such as armed gunmen typically take 5–7 working days because of licence and verification checks.

Yes. Every deployed person is our employee, paid at or above the applicable minimum wage through bank transfer, with monthly EPF and ESIC contributions. Challan copies are attached to your invoice each month so your compliance file is complete without you chasing us — this is the single most common audit gap with security vendors.
