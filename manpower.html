Manpower & Staffing

Housekeeping, drivers, helpers, front-office and skilled staff — recruited, verified, trained and kept on our payroll, working at your site.

What's included

Staff on your site, compliance on our books

You get the people and the output. We carry the recruitment, replacement, payroll and statutory burden.

  • Recruitment and screening — sourcing, interview, skill check, ID and address verification, and reference checks.
  • Full payroll ownership — wages paid by bank transfer at or above minimum wage, with EPF and ESIC contributions every month.
  • Statutory compliance pack — PF/ESIC challans, wage register and attendance summary attached to each invoice.
  • Trained on your process — site induction before day one, so staff are not learning the job in front of your customers.
  • Uniform, ID and consumables — housekeeping staff arrive with uniform, ID and the cleaning schedule already agreed.
  • Leave and absence cover — reliever arranged so your work does not stop when someone takes leave.
  • Supervisor oversight for teams of five or more, with a written monthly performance note.
  • Single monthly invoice per site, with a clear per-role rate and statutory break-up.

Who it's for

Where outsourced staffing pays for itself

You are spending management time on the wrong thing

If a manager is chasing a cleaner's replacement, filing PF challans or re-hiring the same role every quarter, that cost never shows up on an invoice — but it is real.

  • Attrition and re-hiring handled by us, not your HR
  • Leave, absence and festival-season gaps covered by our standby pool
  • One vendor call replaces a dozen internal follow-ups

Your compliance file has holes in it

Contract labour is where audits usually find problems — unpaid PF, missing wage registers, no verification records. Outsourcing only helps if the vendor actually files.

  • EPF and ESIC deposited monthly, challans shared with the invoice
  • Wage register and attendance record maintained and available on demand
  • ID, address and police verification retained for every deployed person

Quality standards

The standard behind every person we place

Anonymised summary of the file we maintain for each deployed staff member — available for your audit at any time.

Role-specific training

Induction on your site's process before day one, plus role training — machine handling for housekeeping, telephone and visitor etiquette for front office, safety briefing for technical trades.

Background verification

Aadhaar and address verification for all staff, police verification for those in resident-facing and high-trust roles, and previous-employer reference checks.

Presentation standard

Uniform and photo ID issued at deployment; grooming and turnout checked at supervisor rounds, which matters most for front-office and customer-facing posts.

Payroll & statutory

On our payroll with bank-transfer wages at or above minimum wage, monthly EPF and ESIC contributions, bonus and leave as per applicable law.
