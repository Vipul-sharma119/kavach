Join as a guard or staff member

We hire continuously across Madhya Pradesh — security guards, supervisors, housekeeping, drivers and skilled staff. Ex-servicemen are particularly welcome.

Why work with us

Paid on time, on the books, with somewhere to go

The security industry has a reputation for late wages and no records. We built this company to be the opposite of that, because the same thing that keeps staff also keeps clients.

  • Wages by bank transfer, at or above minimum wage, paid on a fixed date each month
  • EPF and ESIC from day one — a proper employment record, medical cover for you and your family
  • Uniform, ID card and equipment provided free, not deducted from your wages
  • Paid induction training, plus refresher training every six months
  • Promotion path — guard to senior guard to supervisor to field officer, based on record not favour
  • Posting near your home wherever the roster allows
  • Ex-servicemen welcome — service record counts toward grade and starting pay

What we look for

Security guard

  • Age 21–50
  • Height 168 cm+
  • Class 8 pass
  • Physically fit

No prior experience required — we train. Ex-servicemen and retired police preferred for supervisor roles.

Housekeeping / helper

  • Age 18–55
  • Any gender
  • No formal education bar

Machine-handling experience is an advantage but is taught on the job where needed.

Driver

  • Valid LMV / HMV licence
  • 3+ years driving
  • Clean record

Commercial badge and city route familiarity are an advantage.

Skilled & technical

  • ITI or trade experience
  • Electrical / plumbing / HVAC

Certificate preferred; demonstrated site experience also considered.

Documents to keep ready: Aadhaar card, two passport photos, education certificate if any, previous employer letter if any, bank passbook copy, and discharge book for ex-servicemen. Police verification is arranged by us — you do not pay for it.

Prefer to walk in?

Bring your documents to the head office on any working day between 10 AM and 5 PM. No appointment needed, no fee at any stage.
