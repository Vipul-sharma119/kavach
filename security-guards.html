Security Guards

Trained, police-verified guards for factories, offices, societies, hospitals, retail and events — with supervision you can actually verify.

What's included

What you get with every guard post

A guard is the easy part. What separates a working contract from a failing one is everything around the guard.

  • Police-verified staff — character and antecedent verification completed before deployment, copies on file for your audit.
  • Post-wise duty chart — written instructions for each post, so a reliever knows the job without being retrained.
  • Uniform, ID card and equipment — torch, lathi, whistle, register and, where required, metal detector and safety shoes.
  • Attendance register signed by your site in-charge — the same record your invoice is built from.
  • Supervisor rounds, including surprise night checks, with a visit log left at the site.
  • Named account manager with a direct number — not a general office line.
  • Replacement within 12 hours of any absence, and no billing for uncovered shifts.
  • Monthly compliance pack — attendance summary with PF and ESIC challan copies attached to the invoice.

Roles we deploy

Choose the right post, not just a headcount

Sending five general guards where you needed one supervisor and a gunman is how security budgets get wasted. We'll tell you the mix.

Unarmed security guards

General duty at gates, lobbies, shop floors and parking. Visitor logging, material gate-pass checks, patrolling and shift handover.

  • Gate control
  • Patrolling
  • Visitor log

Armed guards & gunmen

Licensed armed personnel for cash handling, banks, ATMs, jewellery retail and high-value goods movement. Requires 5–7 days lead time for verification.

  • Banks / ATM
  • Cash escort
  • High value

Security supervisors

One supervisor per cluster of posts. Runs shift briefings, checks registers, handles the first level of any incident and reports upward daily.

  • Shift briefing
  • Incident first response

Lady security guards

For frisking points, hospitals, schools, ladies hostels, retail and any site where female visitor or staff checking is required.

  • Frisking
  • Hospitals
  • Institutes

Bouncers & event security

Crowd management for weddings, functions, exhibitions and club venues — entry control, queue management and de-escalation.

  • Events
  • Crowd control
  • Per-shift billing

24×7 control room cover

Round-the-clock coordination desk for multi-post and multi-site clients, with escalation matrix and night-round confirmation calls.

  • Multi-site
  • Escalation matrix

Quality standards

What sits behind each guard on your site

A guard is only as good as the file behind them. This is the standard we hold — and the paperwork we can produce for any deployed person on request.

Induction training

Minimum 40 hours of induction covering post duties, fire safety, first aid basics, register discipline and visitor handling, plus refresher training every six months.

Police verification

Character and antecedent verification completed for 100% of deployed staff, plus Aadhaar, address and previous-employer reference checks.

Uniform & ID standard

Company uniform, photo ID card, shoes, torch, whistle and register issued at deployment. Turnout is checked at every supervisor round.

Employment record

On our payroll with bank-transfer wages at or above minimum wage, monthly EPF and ESIC contributions, and workmen's compensation cover.

Anonymised sample of what sits behind every deployment — the same file is available for any person on your site, on request.
