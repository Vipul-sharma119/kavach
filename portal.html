Client portal

A digital account for existing clients — deployed staff, attendance, invoices and replacement requests in one place. In development; early access opening to contract clients.

Phase 3 · In development

Stop phoning to ask who is on shift

Most security agencies offer no digital account management at all. Attendance lives in a paper register, invoices arrive by email, and every question needs a phone call.

The Kulkavach client portal puts that on a screen: who is deployed at which post right now, live attendance, downloadable invoices with their compliance attachments, and a replacement request that creates a ticket instead of a phone conversation you have to chase.

  • Deployed staff list — names, photos, posts and shift timings for each of your sites
  • Live attendance — who reported, who is absent, and who the reliever is
  • Invoices & compliance — download any month’s invoice with PF/ESIC challans attached
  • Replacement requests — raise a ticket with a reference number and a tracked response time
  • Incident log — supervisor round records and any reported incidents at your site
  • Multi-site view — one login covering every location under your contract
