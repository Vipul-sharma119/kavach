Staff & quality

The people on your site

Absence is escalated the moment the shift-start call goes unanswered. Our supervisor deploys a reliever from the local standby pool — the commitment is a replacement on post within 12 hours, and for critical single-guard posts we aim for the same shift.

You are never billed for an uncovered shift. Absent days are deducted from the monthly invoice and shown as a separate line, not silently absorbed.

Minimum 40 hours of induction before deployment: post duties, patrolling and register discipline, visitor and material gate-pass handling, fire-safety and extinguisher use, first aid basics, emergency escalation and public-handling conduct.

Site-specific briefing happens on top of that, on your premises, before day one — so a guard is not learning your gate procedure while standing at it. Refresher training runs every six months.

Billing & contract

Invoices, payment & paperwork

Standard contracts are billed monthly in arrears, against the attendance register signed by your site in-charge. The invoice shows the per-post rate, actual days worked, overtime if any, statutory contributions and GST as separate lines — so nothing is bundled into one opaque figure.

Short-term and event deployments are billed per shift, usually with part-payment in advance. Payment terms are typically 15–30 days from invoice and can be aligned to your procurement cycle.

Overtime beyond the contracted shift is billed at the statutory rate and only against hours authorised by your site in-charge — we will not invoice overtime nobody approved. National holidays are charged as per the applicable state rules, which we set out in the agreement so there is no month-end surprise.

Only when the statutory minimum wage is revised by the state, which typically happens twice a year. In that case the wage and statutory components move by exactly the notified amount and our service charge stays unchanged — and we share the government notification along with the revised rate. There are no discretionary mid-contract increases.

Every monthly invoice is accompanied by the attendance summary, the wage register extract and EPF and ESIC challan copies for the staff on your site. PSARA licence, GST certificate and insurance documents are provided at onboarding and refreshed on renewal. This is the pack most audits ask for, so we send it by default instead of on request.

Deployed staff are our employees. Workmen’s compensation and ESIC cover sit with us, not with you — that is a large part of why outsourcing to a licensed agency is worth the service charge. Our agreement sets out the indemnity position clearly; your legal team is welcome to review it before signing.

Thirty days’ written notice on either side, no penalty. We will keep staff on post through the full notice period and hand over registers, gate records and any documentation to you or the incoming vendor. We have taken over enough messy transitions to know how damaging a bad exit is — we do not do them.
